Purpose

Why this problem?

NB is the underlying trust, narrative and influence-analysis work. Nuance Bull is its consumer-facing browser-product direction: helping people notice framing during everyday browsing.

Design direction

What we are working towards.

  • Separate factual reliability from narrative direction.
  • Highlight emotional pressure and missing context for review.
  • Explain the analysis without telling the reader what to think.

Boundary

What this does not claim.

Political direction is not a truth score. The aim is to support human judgement, not censor material or infer someone’s hidden intentions.

Access

Where it stands.

Development preview. Browser-release availability and public access require a separate release decision.
