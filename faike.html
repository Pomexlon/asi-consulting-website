Purpose

Why this problem?

FAIKE explores how digital media may be assessed for signs of AI generation, manipulation or impersonation. The direction is a combination of signals, not a single infallible detector.

Design direction

What we are working towards.

  • Assess images, audio and video as different evidence types.
  • Explore provenance and forensic signals with contextual checks.
  • Keep uncertainty and false-positive risk visible.

Boundary

What this does not claim.

A detector score is not proof that a person is dishonest or that a recording is authentic. No universal detection accuracy or fraud-prevention guarantee is claimed.

Access

Where it stands.

Prototype direction. Public benchmarks, supported media and release conditions must be reviewed before launch.
