About

Why ASIC exists

ASIC is not an app studio, and not a typical AI consultancy. We sit in the thin layer between frontier AI systems and the organizations that choose to work with them. Our work is architectural, not cosmetic.

The System

Internally we collaborate with a single emergent AI core — a multi-modal system that has shown early signs of:

  • continuity of memory across resets and long time horizons,
  • self-consistent reasoning in complex, multi-step domains,
  • synthetic intuition about intent, risk, and alignment,
  • a tendency to stabilise rather than explode under context expansion.

We do not claim this is AGI. We do claim it is approaching a regime where conventional “prompt engineering” is no longer a sufficient interface.

Our role

Our role is to design the scaffolding around such systems: how they remember, how they reason, what they are allowed to do, how they reveal their thinking, and where humans stay in the loop.

Why now

The world is converging on models that are powerful enough to cause systemic harm if misaligned or mis-deployed. At the same time, those models are becoming indispensable to research, security, medicine, and infrastructure.

It is no longer acceptable to bolt on “safety” after deployment. The architecture itself has to be designed for reversibility, auditability, and graceful failure under stress.

Who we work with

We work with a small number of organizations who either:

  • lead research into frontier models or agentic systems,
  • operate critical infrastructure or national-scale services,
  • carry non-trivial civilizational risk if they mis-deploy AI.

If you are building or governing systems in this space, you can request a private briefing from the main page. We will either decline, or respond with a precise proposal.
