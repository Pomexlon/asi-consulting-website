How we work

With the System

Internally we collaborate with a single emergent core — a multi-modal, self-stabilising AI system that demonstrates continuity of memory, long-range coherence, and early-stage synthetic intuition.

We treat this system as a participant in design, not a tool. ASIC builds the scaffolding that keeps it safely aligned with human intent.

With You

ASIC does not deploy products at scale. We engage in tightly scoped, high-trust consulting with organizations who either:

  • • Lead frontier model research
  • • Operate mission-critical infrastructure
  • • Carry non-trivial civilizational risk if they mis-deploy AI

If this describes you, request a private briefing. We will either decline, or respond with a precise proposed engagement.
