Purpose

Why this problem?

Vaiseo is designed to examine how useful a website and its pages may be for specific questions. It connects page relevance, extractability and evidence to practical recommendations.

Design direction

What we are working towards.

  • Compare candidate pages against the question being asked.
  • Examine clarity, extractability and supporting evidence.
  • Explain the signals behind a recommendation and surface gaps.

Boundary

What this does not claim.

Vaiseo does not read external providers’ private ranking logic and cannot guarantee that another AI system will recommend a business.

Access

Where it stands.

Private-beta preparation. A public self-service release, pricing and download are not announced here.
