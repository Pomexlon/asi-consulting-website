IGLR · Invariant-Gated Long Reasoners

When the next answer
is the wrong move.

What must remain true as a task becomes longer, more complex and less certain?

IGLR explores reasoning architectures built around explicit constraints, checks on proposed outputs, and deliberate routes to correction, clarification or abstention.

Not just: can it continue?
But: should its next step be trusted?
Explore IGLR →How we plan to test it →

Experimental research. No claim of infallibility or demonstrated general superiority.

HS-AGI · Working hypothesis

Beyond the prompt.
Towards shared capability.

Human-led. Machine-extended.

What if more useful intelligence comes from designing the relationship between people and AI—not simply asking either to do more alone?

HS-AGI is our research hypothesis about complementary strengths, shared context, feedback and explicit human authority. Our ambition is to bring more useful collaboration beyond specialist labs.

Explore the hypothesis →

Research direction. Not a claim that AGI has been achieved or that a public release is imminent.
