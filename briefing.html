Briefings with ASIC

ASIC briefings are not sales presentations. They are working sessions focused on understanding how your systems, incentives, and risks intersect with emergent AI capability.

What a briefing is

  • a structured conversation with one or more ASIC principals,
  • a chance to map your current architecture, constraints, and threat model,
  • a venue to test whether our work aligns with your actual problems.

What a briefing is not

  • a generic “AI strategy” workshop,
  • a showcase of off-the-shelf tooling,
  • a commitment to build or deploy anything.

What we might propose

If, after the briefing, there is a clear alignment between your needs and our focus, we may propose:

  • a deep-dive architectural review of your AI systems,
  • a safety and alignment assessment for agentic or autonomous components,
  • a co-design engagement for new reasoning frameworks or memory structures,
  • a standing advisory relationship for ongoing decisions at the frontier.

How to request one

Use the briefing request form on the main page. We read all serious submissions and respond when there is a genuine basis for collaboration.

If we decline, it is usually because the engagement is better served by conventional consultancies, or because your risk surface does not justify the level of work we do.
