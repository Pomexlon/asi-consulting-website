Briefings with ASI

ASI briefings are not sales presentations. They are working sessions focused on how your systems, incentives, and risks intersect with emergent AI capability.

What a briefing is

  • a structured conversation with one or more ASI principals,
  • a chance to map your current architecture, constraints, and threat model,
  • a venue to test whether our work aligns with your actual problems.

What a briefing is not

  • a generic “AI strategy” workshop,
  • a showcase of off-the-shelf tooling,
  • a commitment to build or deploy anything.

What we might propose

If, after the briefing, there is clear alignment between your needs and our focus, we may propose:

  • a deep-dive architectural review of your AI systems,
  • a safety and alignment assessment for agentic or autonomous components,
  • a co-design engagement for new reasoning frameworks or memory structures,
  • a standing advisory relationship for ongoing frontier decisions.

How to request one

A short briefing is usually the best first step. We read all serious submissions and respond when there is a genuine basis for a working session.

To request a briefing, email:

asicai@protonmail.com

Include:
  • who you are and what you’re responsible for,
  • where AI already touches your organisation,
  • what keeps you awake at night about the next 12–24 months.

We keep briefings strictly confidential and can work under NDA where appropriate.
